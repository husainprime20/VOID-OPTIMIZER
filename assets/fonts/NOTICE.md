The files in this directory are subsets (Latin-1 + typographic punctuation,
kern/liga/tnum features, no hinting) generated with `fonttools` from the
official upstream release. Subsetting is permitted by the OFL; the fonts are
not sold, and the reserved font names are unchanged.
